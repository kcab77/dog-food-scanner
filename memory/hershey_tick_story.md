Kyle stopped giving Hershey Simparica after finding a paralyzed tick on his bed from biting Hershey. He reasoned: if Simparica paralyzes/kills ticks by affecting their nervous system, what is it doing to Hershey's liver long-term? He lives in Massachusetts (top 10 state for tick population) so stopping was terrifying but he took a leap of faith.

He switched to The Resistance by Wildly Blended — a blend of organic compounds designed to make the dog's blood unattractive to ticks (internal repellent).

**The story:** Hershey ran in tall grass for hours hiking in the woods. 5 hours later, Kyle found two ticks (different species) crawling on his bed. Both appeared completely normal — not swollen, not engorged, no signs of feeding. They seemingly never bit Hershey despite being on him for hours in Massachusetts tick country.

**Why:** This is strong real-world evidence that The Resistance works by making blood unattractive to ticks rather than killing them after they bite (like Simparica). The ticks were present but chose not to feed.

**How to apply:** Use this as a testimonial/case study when discussing natural flea/tick protocols, The Resistance, or alternatives to chemical preventatives like Simparica/NexGard.
