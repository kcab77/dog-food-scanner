# 🗺️ PROJECTS — what lives where

This repo holds **four separate products** plus supporting tooling. They share one folder for
historical reasons, which makes it confusing — this file is the map.

> ⚠️ **Why they aren't in four tidy top-level folders:** the repo *root itself* is the PawGrade
> Expo app (EAS builds from here), and `common-sense-dog-ai/` is hardcoded as the Vercel
> "Root Directory" for the live website. Moving either would break iOS builds and take
> commonsensedog.com offline. So the separation is documented here rather than physical.

---
