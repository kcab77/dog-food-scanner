Our Story

From Kibble to Whole Food — Hershey's Journey

Hershey is my 75-lb chocolate Lab. He loves to swim, he's always at the softball fields or the beach — and for the first few years of his life, I thought I was doing everything right. Good vet. Regular shots. Name-brand kibble from the pet store.

Then one day I gave him his Simparica pill. Within hours he was a zombie — glazed over, lethargic, not himself at all. That was the moment I started questioning everything. I dug into the ingredients, the studies, the side effects. What I found scared me. And it sent me down a path I've never come back from.

I was terrified to take him off conventional food and flea prevention. You hear horror stories. You worry you're going to make things worse. But I made the switch — off kibble, off chemical preventatives, onto gently cooked whole food with no added synthetic vitamins or minerals and a natural flea and tick protocol. The difference was immediate. More energy. Better coat. No new lipomas.

That was 7 years ago. I've spent every year since obsessively studying holistic dog health — reading studies, testing protocols, talking to holistic vets. Everything I've learned about Hershey is on this site. And the tool I built so you can check any dog food in seconds is PawGrade.

The Journey

🍗
The Early Years

Trusting the bag

Name-brand kibble. Regular vet visits. Doing everything "right." I had no reason to question any of it — Hershey seemed fine.

😶
The Wake-Up Call

Simparica turned him into a zombie

I gave him his regular Simparica pill and watched him spend the whole day glazed over and lethargic — not himself at all. I started researching what was in it. That was the beginning of everything.

⚠️
Then Came the Lipomas

Fatty lumps that shouldn't be there

His vet said they were "common in Labs." I didn't accept that answer. I started connecting the dots between his diet, the inflammation in his body, and what was growing under his skin.

😰
The Scary Part

Making the switch — terrified

Switching off kibble and off conventional flea prevention felt risky. What if I made things worse? I spent weeks reading everything I could find before I felt confident enough to make the move.

🥩
The Transition

Gently cooked whole food — no synthetic vitamins or minerals

Real ingredients. Nothing on the label I couldn't identify. Within weeks he had more energy than ever. No new lipomas. I knew I'd made the right call.

Today — 7 Years Later

Built PawGrade so you don't have to do this alone

Seven years of obsessive research turned into an app. Scan any dog food barcode and know instantly if it's worth feeding your dog. The research is done. You just point and scan.
