# 📚 Verified sources

**Primary sources that have been fetched and read — not search summaries.**

Every entry below was checked against the original. Where a figure was wrong when first reported,
the correction is shown. **Funding and institution are stated for every study, on every side, as
plain attribution.**

**Rule for adding to this file:** it goes in only after someone has opened the primary source and
read the methods. A source found but not read belongs in `docs/EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md` under "still to
verify," not here.

## 🐕 Lipomas

*Verified 2026-08-20 via storm-research. Full briefing: `storm-reports/canine-lipomas-briefing.html`*

| Source | Good for | Institution / funding |
|---|---|---|
| **[O'Neill et al. 2018, *Canine Genet Epidemiol* 5:9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6161450/)** | **The definitive lipoma epidemiology.** 384,284 dogs. Prevalence 1.94%; age 9–12 **OR 17.52**; bodyweight **OR 1.96**; Labrador 5.15% (OR 2.19); Weimaraner 7.84%; neutered males OR 1.99; insured OR 1.78. ⚠️ Diagnosis was **clinical, not cytological**; BCS unavailable; authors state no causal inference on obesity is possible. | VetCompass, Royal Veterinary College UK. Part-funded by the Kennel Club Charitable Trust. |
| **[Reimann et al. 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10347559/)** | **Canine lipoma cytogenetics — the mechanism mismatch.** n=10; clonal aberrations in 7: trisomy 27, trisomy 13, der(4)(q31). **No HMGA2 involvement.** The human 12q13-15 signature has never been shown in dogs. | Bremen, Germany. Funding not stated. |
| **[Bartuma et al. 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17370328/)** | Human lipoma karyotypes. ⚠️ **Correction:** 83% of 272 lipomas *pre-selected for abnormal karyotype* — not 75% of all lipomas. Sreekantaiah 1991 found abnormality in only ~50% of 109. | Lund University, Sweden. |
| **[Ghisleni et al. 2006, *Vet Clin Pathol* 35(1):24](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16511787/)** | **FNA reliability.** Agreement **90.9%**, sensitivity **89.3%**, PPV **99.4%**, NPV **68.7%**. A "not a tumour" result is wrong ~31% of the time. ⚠️ Mixed 242 dogs **+ 50 cats**, referral population. | University of Milan, Italy. Funding not stated. |
| **[Thompson et al. 2011, *Vet Pathol* 48:156](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21078881/)** | **Subcutaneous MCT masquerading as lipoma.** 306 dogs. Cutaneous grading schemes (Patnaik/Kiupel) were not developed for subcutaneous tumours. Reassuring half: 9% MCT-related death. | University of Guelph, Canada. |
| **[Bergman et al. 1994, *JAVMA* 205(2):322](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7928612/)** | **Infiltrative lipoma.** ⚠️ **Correction:** 16 cases in 15 dogs; of 14 evaluable *tumours*, 5 (36%) recurred, median 239 days. **8 of 15 were Labradors.** | Colorado State University. |
| **[Lamagna et al. 2012, *PLOS ONE* 7(11):e50234](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0050234)** | Intralesional triamcinolone, 15 dogs, 9 complete regressions. ⚠️ **DEMOTED — single-arm case series, not controlled, not randomised, no placebo.** Three later recurrences that "9/15" conceals. 6/15 PU/PD. | University of Naples Federico II. No funding declared. |
| **[Arango et al. 2025, *Front Vet Sci*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12312678/)** | **The only randomised blinded lipoma trial — and it's negative.** 10 dogs, 20 lipomas, within-dog saline control, ultrasound to 12 weeks. Stable size, no QoL benefit, one cellulitis/necrosis. | Funded by Brixton Biosciences, maker of the coolant tested. |
| **[Withers et al. 2018, *Vet Comp Oncol* 16(4)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6234083/)** | **Curcumin doesn't work even IV.** 11 dogs, liposomal curcumin 10 mg/kg **intravenous**, 4 doses. **Zero objective responses.** Says nothing about dietary turmeric — different route entirely. | UC Davis; UCD Center for Companion Animal Health. Lipocurc gifted by SignPath Pharma. |
| **[De Voe et al. 2004, *J Avian Med Surg* 18(1):12](https://doi.org/10.1647/2002-031)** | ⚠️ **The budgerigar study.** The sole evidence behind L-carnitine dosing for canine lipomas. **32 birds**, ~1000 mg/kg **of feed** (a dietary concentration, not a bodyweight dose), 102 days. Authors call it "preliminary." `PubMed "lipoma" AND "carnitine"` → **zero canine results.** | North Carolina State University CVM. |
| **[Conzemius & Evans 2012, *JAVMA* 241:1314](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23113523/)** | **The caregiver placebo effect — use this constantly.** Placebo arm, 58 dogs. **39.7% of owners reported improvement** when force-plate analysis showed none. Vets: 43–45%. | University of Minnesota. |
| **[D'Ettorre et al. 2013](https://www.europeanreview.org/article/4955)** | Familial multiple lipomatosis patients tested insulin-**sensitive**, not resistant. ⚠️ **DEMOTED — n = 4 brothers.** | Catholic University, Rome. |
| **[Berger et al. 2025, *Front Endocrinol*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12705395/)** | Lipoma + metabolic disease association, 7,868 patients. ⚠️ **DEMOTED — no internal controls, no multivariable adjustment, ICD-coded, no pathology.** | Three Israeli hospitals. No funding declared. |

## 🫀 DCM, legumes and taurine

*Verified 2026-08-13*

| Source | Good for | Institution / funding |
|---|---|---|
| **[Journal of Animal Science 2025, skaf225](https://academic.oup.com/jas/article/doi/10.1093/jas/skaf225/8196486)** | **The 18-month DCM trial.** 60 dogs, randomised, double-blind, 4 diets, echo + troponin + NT-proBNP + taurine. No DCM. ⚠️ **0.1% taurine added to every arm** (removes the variable); underpowered by authors' own admission (n=60 vs ~0.5% prevalence). | Hill's Pet Nutrition; all authors current or former Hill's employees. |
| **[PLOS One, 28-day pulse trial](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0285381)** | High-inclusion pulse feeding. 8 beagles, crossover, 42% lentil / 58% pea flour. ⚠️ **28 days cannot detect DCM.** Authors note beagles aren't DCM-predisposed. | Alberta/Manitoba/Ontario/Saskatchewan Pulse Growers, Pulse Canada. |
| **[Dr Judy Morgan, DCM article, Feb 2024](https://drjudymorgan.com/blogs/blog/dilated-cardiomyopathy-and-grain-free-diets-the-saga-continues)** | Her actual stated position: mechanism is *"all speculation,"* the episode *"a huge hoax."* **Use when the app's legume flag needs contextualising against holistic opinion.** | Dr Judy Morgan (sells supplements, courses; formulates the AllProvide Constitutions line). |

## 📋 AAFCO & regulatory

*Verified 2026-08-14 — read from the primary PDF, not a summary*

| Source | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **[AAFCO Dog Food Nutrient Profiles (PFC Final 07/02/14, Midyear 2015)](https://www.aafco.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Model_Bills_and_Regulations_Agenda_Midyear_2015_Final_Attachment_A.__Proposed_revisions_to_AAFCO_Nutrient_Profiles_PFC_Final_070214.pdf)** | **Every minimum and maximum, DM basis and per-1,000-kcal.** Adult protein 18%, fat 5.5%, copper 7.3 mg/kg **with no maximum**, selenium 0.35 min / 2 max, vitamin A 5,000 / 250,000, vitamin D 500 / 3,000, calcium 0.5–2.5% (1.8% large-breed puppy). **Vitamin E rule: +0.6 IU per gram of PUFA.** ⚠️ Note the URL says 2023 but the document is **2014**; the Official Publication is paywalled. | Also: **AAFCO forbids copper oxide** from counting toward the copper minimum — "very poor apparent digestibility." Regulatory backing for the form-matters position. |
| **[AAFCO expert panel on copper](https://www.aafco.org/news/expert-panel-upholds-aafco-guidelines-for-copper-concentrations-in-foods-for-normal-dogs/)** | **Confirms there is still no copper maximum.** Deleted 2007, upheld recently — data for a safe upper limit judged insufficient. Veterinary hepatologists proposed 0.9–1.1 mg/1,000 kcal; not adopted. | ⚠️ Headlines saying officials "reassert copper guidelines" mean they reasserted having **no** maximum. Easy to misread — I did. |

## 🥉 Copper hepatopathy

*Verified 2026-08-13*

| Source | Good for | Institution / funding |
|---|---|---|
| **[JAVMA 2021, 258(4):357](https://avmajournals.avma.org/view/journals/javma/258/4/javma.258.4.357.xml)** | *"Is it time to reconsider current guidelines for copper content in commercial dog foods?"* — the hepatologists' case for a maximum. ⚠️ **Paywalled; abstract only.** | — |
| **[Smedley et al. 2009, *Vet Pathol*](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1354/vp.08-VP-0197-S-FL)** | Hepatic copper in Labradors **higher after the 1997 AAFCO change than before.** ⚠️ **Read as abstract only — needs full-text verification.** | Michigan State (to confirm). |
| **[Copper hepatopathy clinical review](https://todaysveterinarypractice.com/internal-medicine/copper-hepatopathy-in-dogs/)** | **The clinical thresholds.** Normal hepatic copper 120–400 mg/kg DW; >600 potentially harmful; 400–600 manageable by diet. Copper-restricted diets **<0.12 mg/100 kcal (1.2 mg/1,000 kcal)**. Predisposed breeds. **Liver enzymes are NOT sensitive for subclinical stages.** | Today's Veterinary Practice. |

## 🐟 Omega-3, joints and supplements

*Verified 2026-08-13*

| Source | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Randomised, double-blind, multicentre omega-3 OA trial** | **69 mg/kg/day EPA+DHA for 3 months** — significant improvement in pain, lameness, joint disease. Synthesis of 23 randomised trials: efficacy **60–100 mg/kg/day**. NRC safe upper limit 370 mg/kg^0.75. | **This is the number the app had wrong** — it said ~20mg/lb (44 mg/kg), roughly half. |
| **[GLM systematic review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8298224/)** | *"A moderate amount of evidence exists for a scientifically valid relationship"* between GLM and clinical benefit in canine OA — with noted methodological deficiencies in the underlying trials. **Use this exact phrasing; don't upgrade it.** | — |
| **Milk thistle / silymarin in dogs** | Amanita mushroom poisoning: **IV** silymarin — ~30% of untreated controls died, none of the treated. Twedt et al. 2003: oral silybin-phospholipid raised liver glutathione. ⚠️ **The dramatic result is IV for acute poisoning, not a daily capsule.** No large canine RCT for routine use. | Route-of-administration trap — a worked example. |

## 🔥 Lifespan & caloric restriction

*Verified 2026-08-20 — and this one has been wrong in the app twice*

| Source | Good for | Institution / funding |
|---|---|---|
| **[Kealy et al. 2002, *JAVMA* 220(9):1315](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11991408/)** | **The strongest finding in canine nutrition.** 48 littermate-paired Labradors, 25% restriction from 8 weeks to death. Median lifespan **13.0 vs 11.2 years**. BCS 4.6 vs 6.7. Osteoarthritis treatment delayed **13.3 vs 10.3 years**. ⚠️ **12.9/11.1 is a transcription error in circulation — 12.9 is the control group's MAXIMUM, not its median.** | Nestlé Purina Research Facility, Gray Summit, Missouri. Funded by Purina. |
| **[Lawler et al. 2008, *Br J Nutr* 99(4):793](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114507871686)** | Same cohort, two decades. Neoplasia involved 23% of deaths; restriction **delayed** death from malignancy (11.6 vs 9.7 years, not significant) rather than preventing it. **Benign mammary tumours were tabulated; the word "lipoma" appears nowhere in either paper.** | Purina, with Pennsylvania, Illinois, Michigan State. |

## ⚠️ Sources that turned out to be weaker than they looked

Keep these visible — they're the ones most likely to be quoted at you.

- **BioSpecifics collagenase canine lipoma data** — 4 enrolled, **3 evaluable**, company **press release**, never published or peer-reviewed. [Link](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/biospecifics-announces-positive-results-from-clinical-trial-in-canine-lipomas-113036459.html)
- **The L-carnitine lipoma dose** — [veterinarysecrets.com, 22 Jan 2025](https://veterinarysecrets.com/dog-lipomas-natural-remedies/), 50 mg/kg BID. **Derived from budgerigars. Zero canine studies exist.**
- **"75% of lipomas carry HMGA2 rearrangement"** — wrong denominator, and **never demonstrated in dogs.**
- **Judy Morgan on bloat** — [*"kibble that blows apart… can be dangerous"*](https://drjudymorgan.com/blogs/blog/kick-kibble-to-the-curb-bloating-and-cystitis), **27 Sept 2016.** Gas analysis of bloated stomachs finds essentially room air — aerophagia, not swelling. **Her recommendation (drop kibble) still matches the app's evidence; her mechanism doesn't.**
