# 📹 Scan videos — 14 days of content

**The format:** one popular bag, one scan, one surprising line. The scan result IS the content.

**Why this beats every other format:** it matches what people already search — *"is Blue Buffalo good
for dogs"* is a brand name plus a question, and it has real volume. **And it creates the exact itch
the app scratches** — they can't help wondering what their bag scores.

---

## ⚠️ Read this before you film

**Only one hook below is verified.** I read Purina's own label PDF for Pro Plan; that one you can
say on camera as printed.

**For everything else: scan the actual bag first and read what comes up.** Do not say a number or an
ingredient I haven't confirmed — formulas get reformulated constantly, and being wrong on camera
about a named brand is the one thing that can actually hurt you. **The app is the fact-checker. Use it.**

---

## The script (same every time — that's the point)

```
[0-3s]   HOOK — say the brand and the surprise. No intro, no "hey guys."
[3-8s]   THE SCAN — film the phone scanning the bag. Show the score land.
[8-20s]  THE ONE LINE — the single most interesting flag. Not all of them. One.
[20-27s] THE FAIRNESS BEAT — what the bag actually does right.
[27-30s] THE TURN — "Scan yours. Link in bio."
```

**The fairness beat is not optional.** It's what separates you from every fear-mongering pet account,
and it's why people trust the score instead of arguing with it. Every bag has something right.

**Never say "this food is bad."** Say what's in it and what the score was. The bag speaks for itself.

## Day 1 — the one to open with

### 🥇 Purina Pro Plan Sensitive Skin & Stomach, Salmon & Rice

**The best-selling dog food on Amazon.** ~$3.43M/month in revenue, February 2026.

**✅ VERIFIED HOOK — from [Purina's own label PDF](https://www.purina.com/sites/default/files/product-label-deck-file/2024-10/4449_m444922_pro_plan_sensitive_skin_stomach_salmon_rice_formula_dog_food_dry_kk7.pdf):**
the ingredient list contains **menadione sodium bisulfite complex** — synthetic vitamin K3.

> *"This is the number one best-selling dog food in America. Real salmon is the first ingredient —
> that part's true. Now let me show you what's near the bottom of the same label."*

**The fairness beat, and say it:** salmon genuinely is first, the fat is **preserved with mixed
tocopherols rather than BHA**, and it has **no corn, wheat or soy**. That's a better label than most
of the shelf. **Menadione is the one thing worth knowing about**, and the app's own text is careful
here — the FDA banned it from human supplements, and there's no published report of harm in dogs at
food levels. Say both halves.

**Why this is your strongest opener:** it's the biggest seller, the fact is verified, and you're not
attacking the food — you're showing someone something on their own bag they never read.

## The three videos most likely to travel

**1. The price flip (film this second, day 2).**
> *"This bag is $62. This one is $28. Watch."*

Kirkland versus a premium bag. **If the cheap one wins, that's the video that goes furthest** — it's
counterintuitive, it's genuinely useful, and it helps people who can't spend more. Which is exactly
who you said you built this for.

**2. Same brand, two bags.**
> *"Same brand. Same shelf. Twelve-point difference."*

Purina ONE versus Pro Plan, or Blue Buffalo Life Protection versus Wilderness. Shows the score is
reading the label, not the logo.

**3. Hershey.**
His lipomas, what he eats now, what he ate for six years before. **You have something nobody else in
this space has: a real dog, a real problem, and eight years of receipts.** Not every day — but this
is what makes people follow rather than just watch.

---

## Two rules that protect you

**Don't punch down on the cheap bags.** Pedigree and Dog Chow are what a lot of people can afford,
and contempt is how you lose the exact audience you want. The line is *"here's the best bag at this
price"* — never *"you're poisoning your dog."* **Your whole positioning is "I fed my dog kibble for
six years, I'm not judging you." Don't sand that off for engagement.**

**Scan before you script.** Every hook except Pro Plan's menadione is unverified until you run it.
Formulas change. Being wrong about a named brand on camera is the one mistake that costs you real
credibility — and it's completely avoidable, because you built the thing that checks.
